About the data

How to read the charts

  • Keep the population and period visible. Annual participation, daily reach and preferences among existing listeners answer different questions.
  • Keep formats and industry measures separate. People can use several formats. Publisher revenue, subscriptions and audience percentages are different measures.
  • Preserve gaps and survey changes. Unpublished years stay missing. Different survey bases do not create a valid country ranking.
  • Check the source. Observation years, report dates, exact table or page locators and original definitions stay attached to the evidence.
