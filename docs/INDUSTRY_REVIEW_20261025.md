## Reviewed source families

[AJPEA paper books and electronic book/comic sales 2022-2025](https://shuppankagaku.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/%E3%83%8B%E3%83%A5%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AA%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B92601.pdf): All selected years use one 2026 winter report vintage. Currency is JPY; original100-million-JPY units rescaled to JPY million without FX conversion. Paper magazines and electronic magazines excluded. Audiobooks not identified. Price/tax basis is not specified in public release. No country ranking or inferred growth.

[KVB Boekwerk 2025 retail market monitor monetary history](https://kvbboekwerk.nl/monitor/markt/verkoopcijfers-2025): EUR million gross general-book retail sales values. Includes individually purchased Dutch ebooks and some monitored non-book categories; excludes subscriptions, streaming and publisher non-retail sales.2025 panel expansion revised2023 onward; do not infer continuous growth from2022. Monitor updated in February 2026; exact update day is not stated.

[French SNE publisher revenue, 2025–2026 report vintage](https://www.sne.fr/app/uploads/2026/06/SNE-Synthese-Statistique-2026-WEB-v-def.pdf): Publisher net revenue including rights and book-sales-only revenue are separate overlapping series. All extracted values from the same 2025–2026 vintage. Not retail consumer spending, not audience participation, and not a separate audiobook revenue measure. The report and release differ in editorial-brand count; do not treat that count as a measured population denominator. Export activity is included in the publisher-origin scope, not confined to purchases by French residents; PDF page 22 uses the same net book-sales total when discussing exports.

[German book trade turnover, 2026 table vintage](https://www.boersenverein.de/fileadmin/bundesverband/dokumente/presse/digitale_pressemappen/WIPK/Tabellenkompendium_2026.pdf): Estimated turnover at final consumer prices, not publisher net receipts. Same 2026 vintage for 2022–2025. No derived country ranking, foreign currency conversion, market share or audiobook revenue inference. The exact table 2025 value is EUR 9619 million; the press release rounds to EUR9.62billion.

[Spain domestic book market billings, FGEE 2025 advance](https://federacioneditores.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Avance-Resultados-Comercio-Interior-2025-110626.pdf): Original EUR million in one June 2026 vintage. All-format billings overlap their paper, electronic book and audiobook components. Audiobooks are separately published and not a digital or broader-audio proxy. Exact price/tax basis is not separately stated in this public advance, so do not equate amounts with net publisher receipts. 2025 electronic table precision174.387 differs from rounded summary174.39; retained without forcing components to match total. No inferred growth or audience share.

[Brazil printed-book publisher sales, 2025 report vintage](https://www.cbl.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Producao_e_Vendas_anobase_2025_imprensa-1.pdf): Same 2025 report vintage contains 2024/2025 nominal BRL values. Publisher receipts are not retail expenditure. Printed books only. Market and government add to total and are overlapping display alternatives. Separate June 2026 historical report uses constant 2025 prices and was not merged. No audiobook quantity extracted from combined digital content.

[Canada book publishers operating revenue, February 2026 table release](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=2110020001): Current table vintage accessed25 October 2026; release 5 February 2026. CAD million operating revenue across formats, not audiobook-specific.2022 revised; most recent 2024 preliminary and subject to revision. Operating revenue excludes investment income, capital gains, extraordinary gains and nonrecurring items. No observations interpolated into odd years. The industry code changes from 511130 to 513130 starting 2022.

[Swedish Book Sales Statistics 2025, retail and streaming revenue by format](https://forlaggare.se/bokforsaljningsstatistik/): Retail and streaming turnover excluding VAT, not publisher receipts. Same 2025 report vintage covers 2022-2025. Domestic-publication streamed audio and ebooks are separated; foreign digital is combined and excluded from both format-specific series. Panel revisions change earlier vintages; no international ranking or cross-source audio shares.

[Finnish Publishers Association annual member publisher sales 2025](https://www.epressi.com/tiedotteet/kustannustoiminta/kustantajien-tilasto-painettujen-tietokirjojen-myynti-kaantyi-nousuun-viime-vuonna.html): Member publisher net sales, not retail market value or audience size. Annual and quarterly collections are not directly comparable. Only 2025 broad total validated. Audiobook numbers remain excluded pending reconciliation of live HTML and official Excel export.

[The Global Publishing Industry in 2022: historical revenue observations](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/pubdocs/en/wipo-pub-1064-2023-2-en-the-global-publishing-industry-in-2022.pdf): Historical 2022 observations from the 2023 publication, checked 25 October 2026. Original eleven project countries were excluded to avoid duplicate incompatible series. Trade-only, print-only, French-speaking-Belgium and retail-price exceptions are explicit. USD amounts were converted by WIPO using period-average IFS rates; the project performs no FX conversion. Publication precision is year only. Digital/audio figures elsewhere in the report combine formats and are not treated as audiobook receipts. No country ranking, annual growth or missing-year estimate is produced.

## Definitions and exclusions

Publisher receipts, retail turnover and operating revenue describe different parts of the industry. WIPO already converted its selected 2022 amounts to USD using Annex A exchange rates. The project performs no further currency conversion. The WIPO publication date remains 2023 at year precision, without an invented month or day. These are historical observations, not current market estimates.

Belgium’s WIPO figure covers its French speaking region. Trade only, print only and retail price exceptions remain in each source row. The 2021 rows for Mexico and the Republic of Korea were excluded from this observation window.

France includes domestic and export publisher activity. Swedish figures cover participating retail and streaming channels; its separate streamed audio and ebook categories exclude foreign digital publications, which the source combines. Spanish audiobook amounts are directly published, not calculated from shares.

Canada supplies 2022 and 2024 only. Its 2022 amount is revised and 2024 is preliminary. Brazilian nominal annual values stay separate from inflation adjusted history. The Dutch retail panel was revised from 2023, leaving a panel break against 2022. Japanese table units of 100 million JPY were multiplied by 100 for display in JPY million; this is unit scaling, not FX conversion.

Finnish audiobook observations remain excluded pending clarification. The live HTML reports EUR 43.360 million for 2025 while its official Excel export reports EUR 43.610 million. The cause is unresolved. Confirmed overall 2025 member publisher net sales are included. Both conflicting locators remain in the review metadata.

No missing year was estimated. Company records overlap by parent and brand and are not added to industry values. Every observation retains its URL, exact locator, scope, currency, publication date and report vintage.
