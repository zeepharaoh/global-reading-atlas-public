# Public source update, 25 October 2026

The reviewed genre history now contains 203 observations from nine countries. The separately labelled 2026 Storytel listener study adds five further countries. Together the genre views cover 14 countries, with each source and question kept separate.

Publication dates remain separate from observation years. The Australian report was released in June 2026 and its detailed tables in October 2026, but the latest survey observations are from 2025. The Dutch study contains actual 2026 observations. No unpublished annual values are estimated.

Original source links, exact page or table references, populations, recall periods, report vintages and extraction decisions are retained in [the review record](../data/curated/review_20261025.json) and [the source register](../data/source_register.json). New figures use original genre labels. Reader preferences, population participation and publisher revenue remain different measures.

## Evidence held for further review

The Dutch report's purchase genre table repeats percentages across unrelated categories. Its narrative and rounded chart components also imply different totals for any audiobook listening. Those genres and reconstructed totals were excluded; the explicit weekly segments were accepted.

The Spain report's headline audiobook total is retained as published. Its component percentages use different precision. Full fieldwork and sampling mode details were unavailable in the hosted copy, and earlier wave designs were not independently revalidated. No growth rate is calculated from this series.

The 2026 Ofcom report has audiobook evidence, but direct PDF and spreadsheet retrieval returned access errors and the numeric chart mapping could not be verified. New Zealand's 2025 report uses different audiobook bases across its summary and detailed questions. Both remain documented sources without imported numeric observations. Brazil's original publisher download redirected to a suspended website; no new genre values were accepted.

The automated refresh checked existing source and company URLs and refreshed supported APIs. Simon & Schuster and Scholastic returned HTTP 403; their last reviewed records were preserved. Changed company and discovery pages remain flagged for release review. A successful download alone does not approve an extract.

Pew, Nordicom, BookNet Canada, APA and the existing Storytel study were separately rechecked against their current original evidence. The existing validated figures were retained. [Review receipts](../data/curated/core_review_20261025.json) describe the precise scope of those checks.
